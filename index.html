The Process

How QuantumMark Works

Six precise steps from raw image to cryptographically certified, quantum-watermarked asset.

01

Upload Image

Submit a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image through the secure upload interface.

02

Generate Quantum Key

A 256-bit key is generated from a quantum entropy source for this image alone.

03

Embed Watermark

The mark is encoded into the image's frequency domain — invisible to the eye.

04

Analyze Security

Run integrity analysis and compute SNR scores across all image blocks.

05

Verify

Confirm the watermark is intact, detect any tamper attempts with confidence scores.

06

Generate Certificate

Issue a signed ownership certificate with the quantum key fingerprint and timestamp.

About

What Is QuantumMark?

QuantumMark is an academic research platform exploring the intersection of quantum cryptography and digital image forensics. Our goal is to produce open, reproducible tooling for the scientific community — enabling researchers to protect, trace, and certify digital media with the highest security guarantees available.

The project is active, open to collaboration, and currently seeking peer review. If you work in cryptography, signal processing, or digital rights management, we'd love to hear from you.
